#+title: V11 parameter provenance audit

* Purpose

This file classifies parameters before they are allowed into the V11 dynamic
model. V11 does not inherit historical defaults merely because they exist in
CathodeParameters.

* Accepted without calibration

** Fundamental constants

- Faraday constant F = 96485.33212 C/mol.
- Universal gas constant R = 8.31446261815324 J/mol/K.

** Ballard manual / stack specification

Source module:

  src/pemfc_dedalus/ballard_1020acs.py

Currently admissible examples include:

- cell count when explicitly tied to the user's stack configuration;
- thermal mass per cell from the manual;
- bus-plate resistance;
- current and cell-voltage operating limits;
- oxidant temperature and humidity limits;
- hydrogen pressure range;
- purge volume per cell;
- purge interval in A.s;
- purge duration/rate limits.

The manual BOL polarization curve is VALIDATION DATA, not a constitutive law
for predicting V11 voltage.

* Literature constitutive relations already present

The following relations may be used when their material assumptions match the
membrane actually modelled and their validity range is respected:

- Springer water-content/activity relation;
- Springer proton conductivity;
- Motupally Nafion water diffusivity;
- electro-osmotic drag relation n_d = lambda/22;
- Ge et al. interfacial water-transfer relation at its stated temperature;
- Grimaldi/Olesen water-transport relations where their stated material model
  is applicable.

Their literature constants are not to be re-fitted against Ballard voltage,
temperature or purge trajectories.

* Currently unresolved quantities needed for full voltage prediction

** Active electrochemical area

Required to convert imposed stack current I into current density j = I/A.

The product manual itself gives only external stack dimensions, but two
independent literature sources specific to the FCgen-1020ACS report:

  A_cell = 0.0145 m2/cell = 145 cm2/cell.

This value is therefore accepted for V11 as a literature-sourced geometric
parameter, not a fitted quantity.

At I = 26.04 A:

  j = I / A_cell
    = 26.04 / 0.0145
    ~= 1795.86 A/m2.

The historical V10 reference value 2150 A/m2 must not be carried into V11 as a
physical current-density target because it corresponds to a smaller implied
area (~0.01211 m2) and was not the reported 1020ACS active area.

Status: RESOLVED FROM LITERATURE.

** Exchange-current density / catalyst kinetic parameters

Required by Butler-Volmer activation kinetics.

The historical volumetric j0 is explicitly a calibration parameter and is not
accepted.

Status: UNRESOLVED pending independently sourced electrode/catalyst data.

** Reversible potential model

A fixed E_eq is not sufficient for the dynamic model. V11 requires a
thermodynamic reversible-potential relation based on temperature and species
activities/partial pressures.

Status: TO IMPLEMENT from thermodynamics.

** Electronic/contact resistances

Bus-plate resistance is available from the Ballard manual. Other cell,
bipolar-plate, GDL and contact resistances require geometry/material data or
published independently applicable properties.

Status: PARTLY RESOLVED.

* Rule

If one of these unresolved quantities is required by a calculation, V11 should
raise an explicit error or require it as an argument. It must not substitute a
historical fitted default.


* Additional parameters recovered from MAN5100319-0B

** Anode gas volume

Section 6.3.2 states that a purge volume of 20 mL/cell is equivalent to two
anode volumes.

Therefore:

  V_anode = 10 mL/cell.

For the 10-cell user stack:

  V_anode,stack = 100 mL = 1.0e-4 m3.

This replaces the historical V0.7 provisional assumption of 20 mL/cell for
V11.

** Purge flow and timing

Run-time purge:

  minimum purge flow = 2.4 slpm/cell
  purge volume = 20 mL/cell
  purge duration < 500 ms
  purge interval = 2300 A.s.

Ballard lab run-time condition:

  20 mL/cell
  200 ms
  2300 A.s.

Start-up purge:

  purge flow = 6.0 slpm/cell
  purge duration = 200 ms
  purge volume = 20 mL/cell.

For 10 cells:

  run-time purge flow >= 24 slpm
  start-up purge flow = 60 slpm
  purge volume = 200 mL.

At I = 26.04 A:

  purge period = 2300 / 26.04 = 88.33 s.

** Anode pressure drop

The manual gives:

  Delta P_anode [mbar]
    = 31.21 * H2 flow per cell [slpm].

Therefore:

  at 2.4 slpm/cell: Delta P ~= 74.9 mbar
  at 6.0 slpm/cell: Delta P ~= 187.3 mbar.

** Hydrogen reaction flow

The appendix gives:

  H2 reacted = 0.00696 slpm/(A cell) * I * n_cells.

At 26.04 A and 10 cells:

  H2 reacted ~= 1.8124 slpm.

This agrees with the Faraday-law implementation and can be used as a manual
cross-check, not as a fitted law.

** Stoichiometric oxidant flow

The appendix gives:

  air required = 0.0166 slpm/(A cell) * I * n_cells.

At 26.04 A and 10 cells:

  stoichiometric air ~= 4.3226 slpm.

The manual requires steady-state air stoichiometry greater than 20, implying a
strict oxidant floor above approximately:

  86.45 slpm

at this operating point.

The cooling requirement is generally much larger than the oxidant requirement
and must be determined from the energy balance rather than from oxygen
stoichiometry alone.

** Bus-plate electrical loss

The manual gives total bus-plate resistance:

  R_bus ~= 2.2 mOhm.

At 26.04 A:

  Delta V_bus ~= 57.3 mV

for the complete stack bus plates.

** Heat-generation relation

The manual gives:

  Q_created = n_cells * I * (1.253 - V_cell).

At the nominal 10-cell, 26.04 A, V_cell ~= 0.76817 V point:

  Q_created ~= 126.25 W.

This relation is admissible as a direct manufacturer-provided energy balance
for V11.

** Air properties

The appendix tabulates dry-air density, heat capacity and thermal conductivity
at 1 atm over 0--100 degC. These values may be interpolated when the V11
thermal/air model is implemented.

** Cathode pressure drop

The manual provides an explicit stack pressure-drop correlation as a function
of stack temperature, stack volumetric flow and cell count.

The parsed equation formatting is ambiguous enough that the equation should be
transcribed from the rendered manual page before implementation.

* Still not recoverable from the manual

The manual does not provide enough information to determine without additional
assumptions:

- true electrochemically active cell area;
- catalyst loading / ECSA;
- exchange-current density j0;
- GDL/MPL/CL porosity and tortuosity;
- membrane thickness and equivalent weight;
- N2 crossover permeability;
- detailed contact resistances inside the cell.

These remain unresolved or require independent literature/material data.


* Literature cross-check: reduced-order FCgen-1020ACS model

A 2025 Concordia MASc thesis devoted to a reduced-order dynamic model of the
Ballard FCgen-1020ACS provides a useful provenance audit.

It reports as non-fitted parameters:

- active area A_cell = 1.45e-2 m2;
- stack heat capacity = 100 J/K per cell;
- dry membrane density = 1980 kg/m3;
- membrane equivalent molar mass = 1.1 kg/mol;
- ionomer volume fraction in CL = 0.3;
- membrane ionomer volume fraction = 1;
- CL thickness = 1e-5 m;
- GDL thickness = 2e-4 m;
- membrane thickness = 8e-5 m;
- GDL porosity = 0.76;
- CL porosity = 0.4;
- intrinsic permeability ~1e-12 m2 (GDL), ~1e-13 m2 (CL);
- contact angle = 110 deg.

These values are literature-based rather than Ballard-manual measurements and
must retain that provenance distinction.

The same thesis explicitly marks the cathode exchange-current parameter,
cathode concentration exponent and cathode transfer coefficient as parameters
that require fitting to experimental data for that specific system.

Therefore V11 SHALL NOT adopt those fitted kinetic parameters as predictive
defaults merely because they appear in that model.

The thesis also gives literature reference values (Neyerlin et al.) for ORR
kinetics, but explicitly notes that electrode-material differences require
adjustment for the actual stack. Those values may be used for sensitivity or
bounds only until stack-specific catalyst information is independently sourced.


* ORR kinetics: literature closure added

V11 now contains a catalyst-specific ORR kinetic reference based on
Neyerlin et al. for high-surface-area Pt/C.

Accepted literature parameters, referenced to the reversible-potential
(zero-overpotential) formulation:

- reference temperature:
    T_ref = 353 K;
- reference oxygen partial pressure:
    p_O2,ref = 101.3 kPa;
- Pt-area-specific exchange current density:
    i0,Pt,ref ~= 2.5e-8 A/cm2_Pt;
- cathodic transfer coefficient:
    alpha_c ~= 1;
- oxygen reaction order:
    gamma ~= 0.54;
- activation energy:
    E_a ~= 67 kJ/mol.

The implemented temperature/oxygen dependence is:

  i0,Pt(T,pO2)
    = i0,Pt,ref
      * (pO2/p_O2,ref)^gamma
      * exp[-E_a/R * (1/T - 1/T_ref)].

The cathodic Tafel loss is then evaluated on geometric cell area.

** Critical geometric conversion still unresolved for FCgen-1020ACS

The Neyerlin exchange current is defined per electrochemically active Pt area,
not per geometric cell area.

The required conversion is:

  roughness factor
    = A_Pt,electrochemical / A_geo
    = 10 * L_Pt[mg/cm2_geo] * ECSA[m2_Pt/g_Pt].

Then:

  i0,geo = i0,Pt * roughness factor.

The FCgen-1020ACS product documentation and literature reviewed so far do not
provide sufficiently defensible stack-specific values for:

- cathode Pt loading L_Pt;
- catalyst ECSA.

Therefore these quantities remain mandatory explicit inputs. V11 SHALL NOT
silently use generic values such as 0.4 mgPt/cm2 and 50 m2Pt/gPt as Ballard
defaults.

Values of that type may appear only in unit-conversion tests or sensitivity
studies, with their non-Ballard provenance stated explicitly.

Status:

- Pt/C intrinsic ORR kinetics: RESOLVED FROM LITERATURE;
- FCgen-1020ACS catalyst roughness factor: UNRESOLVED.


* FCgen-1020ACS catalyst-layer roughness audit

A targeted search was performed for FCgen-1020ACS / FCgen-1020AVS catalyst
loading and electrochemically active surface area.

Findings:

- FCgen-1020ACS/1020AVS publications identify porous carbon electrodes and
  Pt-based catalyst layers but do not report a defensible stack-specific ECSA
  or cathode Pt loading suitable for direct use in V11.
- Historical Ballard/Johnson-Matthey work reports cathode loadings around
  0.55--0.60 mgPt/cm2 for earlier Ballard stack hardware.
- Those historical values are not sufficient evidence that the FCgen-1020ACS
  uses the same cathode loading, and they therefore SHALL NOT become V11
  defaults.

Status remains:

- cathode Pt loading for FCgen-1020ACS: UNRESOLVED;
- FCgen-1020ACS catalyst ECSA: UNRESOLVED;
- roughness factor: UNRESOLVED.

V11 voltage prediction must continue to require Pt loading and ECSA as explicit
inputs until a product-specific source is found.



* N2 crossover literature closure

The FCgen-1020ACS manual does not provide N2 permeability. V11 therefore uses
an independently published Nafion 117 gas-permeability relation rather than the
historical fitted crossover flux.

Accepted reference: Catalano et al. (2012), International Journal of Hydrogen
Energy 37(7), 6308-6316, DOI 10.1016/j.ijhydene.2011.07.047.

V11 uses the following literature anchors already audited in the V07 studies:

- dry N2 permeability at 35 degC: 0.18 Barrer;
- dry-Nafion N2 permeation activation energy: 49.6 kJ/mol;
- approximately 95x permeability increase at water activity 0.75;
- reported high-humidity enhancement bounded at approximately 100x;
- partial molar water volume: 17 cm3/mol.

These values are not fitted to FCgen-1020ACS behavior.

The V11 closure combines them with the V11 membrane thickness, active area,
density, equivalent weight, mean membrane water content and instantaneous N2
partial-pressure gradient.

Status: RESOLVED FROM LITERATURE AS A LUMPED-MEMBRANE CLOSURE.

Caveat: the Catalano water-content dependence is a material relation, while
V11 currently represents membrane hydration by one mean lambda. A dynamic
through-plane lambda(z) state would be required to replace this one-point
reduction by a resistance integral without inventing an internal profile.
